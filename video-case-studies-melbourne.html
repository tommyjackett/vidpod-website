FAQ

Something else on your mind? Ask us anything.

What's the difference between a case study and a testimonial video?

A testimonial is your customer in their own words, short and personal, saying why they'd recommend you. A case study tells the full story: the problem, what you changed and the result. We can make both from the same interview, so you get a quick one for social and the deeper story for sales.

Does my customer need to prepare anything?

Only as much as they'd like. Some customers love to be across everything, so our producers can speak with them directly or give you a run sheet and a clear overview of what we'll film to pass on. On the day our team makes them feel comfortable, and that's where the best stories come from.

What do we actually get?

A polished two to three minute video that tells the story properly. We also make shorter versions to suit where you'll use them, like social media, your website or sales presentations.

Can one project give us more than one video?

Yes. Every interview holds more great moments than one video can use, so we plan for that from the start and keep the best of them in your content bank. You can keep drawing on it for social posts, sales and new videos long after the main film is out.

Where do you film?

Wherever the story is best told: at your customer's place, at yours, or both. How long we spend comes down to what your customer needs, what the story needs and what's possible creatively within your budget. We're based in Melbourne and travel interstate.

Can you make a series of case studies?

Yes. Whether it's a few customers or a whole network, we build a suite of stories with the same look and structure, so they work together on your website, in sales and on social.
