FAQ

Something else on your mind? Ask us.

What's the difference between a case study and a testimonial video?

A testimonial is your customer saying why they'd recommend you. A case study tells the full story: the challenge, how you helped and the result. We can make both from the one day of filming.

Does my customer need to prepare anything?

Nothing at all. We send them a simple outline beforehand, then on the day it's a relaxed conversation. We guide them the whole way, and most people are comfortable within a few minutes.

What do we actually get?

A polished two to three minute video that tells the story properly. We also make shorter versions to suit where you'll use them, like social media, your website or sales presentations.

Can one shoot give us more than one video?

Yes. A good conversation always has more great moments than one video can hold. We keep the best of them, so you can share more stories and social posts over time.

Where do you film?

Wherever your customer is. Usually it's half a day with them, and we can also film at your place to show your team and work. We're based in Melbourne and travel interstate.
