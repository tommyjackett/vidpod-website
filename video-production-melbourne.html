FAQ

Something else on your mind? Ask us anything.

What does VIDPOD do?

VIDPOD is a Melbourne video and podcast production company, founded by Tommy Jackett. We make brand films, case studies, explainers and social content, and take each one from the first chat to the finished video. You deal with one team the whole way, so nothing gets lost between people.

What kinds of videos do you make?

Brand films, branded content, explainers and animation, social content and customer case studies. Most projects give you more than one video: a main piece, plus shorter versions ready for your website, socials and presentations.

How does a video project work?

It starts with a short chat about what the video needs to do and who it's for. We plan it with you, film it and edit it, and you review each version before anything is final. You always know what's happening next and what we need from you, which is usually not much.

Can you film at our office, event or conference?

Yes. We film at offices, sites, stores and events, and we bring everything needed to make it look and sound great. We work around your day, so your team can get on with theirs.

Do you work outside Melbourne?

Yes. We're based in Melbourne and work with organisations right across Australia. We travel interstate whenever the job needs it and plan each day so you get the most from it.

How much does a video cost?

Every project is different, so we quote on what you actually need: how many videos, where we film and how they'll be used. Tell us about the project and we'll send a clear quote before anything starts, with no surprises.
