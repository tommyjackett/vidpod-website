Video production, Melbourne

VIDPOD is a Melbourne video production company run by founder Tommy Jackett. We make brand films, case study videos told as stories, and event and conference content for organisations across Australia, from the first idea through to the final edit.

We've been doing this for over a decade, for brands like @Pilates, Border Express, Focus Insights, Quad Lock and Country Road. You deal with one person the whole way, and you end up with videos your audience actually wants to watch.

At a glance

  • Who: VIDPOD, founded by Tommy Jackett
  • What: brand films, case study videos, event and conference content
  • Where: based in Melbourne, working with organisations across Australia
  • Who for: marketing and comms teams at brands, universities, financial services firms and franchise networks

What we make

Brand films

A film that shows people who you are and why you do what you do. Founding stories, brand videos, company overviews and launch films, made to live on your website, open a presentation or run across social.

Case study videos, told as stories

Not a testimonial. A real customer talks through a specific problem and how it was solved, so the people you want to reach can see themselves in it. We find the story in the interview and shape it into a film. They work even better as a series.

Event and conference content

We film your conference, launch or event and turn it into content you can keep using long after the day: a highlight film, keynote and session edits, interviews with speakers and guests, and short clips for social.

What you get

  • One point of contact from brief to delivery
  • Ideas, creative concept, planning and scripting
  • Filming with cinema cameras, lighting and professional audio
  • The full edit: story, music, graphics and colour
  • Versions for wherever it's going: wide for your website and YouTube, vertical and square for social
  • Final files ready to upload

FAQ

What does VIDPOD do?

VIDPOD is a video and podcast production company in Melbourne, founded by Tommy Jackett. On the video side we make brand films, case study videos and event and conference content for organisations across Australia.

How much does a video cost?

Every project is different, so we quote each one on what you actually need: how many videos, shoot days, locations and versions. Tell us about the project and we'll send you a clear quote before anything starts.

What's the difference between a case study video and a testimonial?

A testimonial is a customer saying nice things about you. A case study is a story: a real customer talks through a specific problem and how it was solved. It's more believable, and it's what we recommend.

Can you film at our office, event or conference?

Yes. We film on location at offices, sites, stores and events. We bring the cameras, lighting and audio, so all you need is the space.

How long does a video take?

It depends on the project. We lock in a delivery date at the start and plan back from it, so if you've got a launch, event or campaign date, let us know early.

Do you work outside Melbourne?

We're based in Melbourne and work with organisations across Australia. We travel interstate when the job needs it.

Got a video in mind?

Tell us what you're after. No brief is too rough.
