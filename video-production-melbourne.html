FAQ

What does VIDPOD do?

VIDPOD is a video and podcast production company in Melbourne, founded by Tommy Jackett. On the video side we make brand films, case study videos and event and conference content.

How much does a video cost?

Every project is different, so we quote each one on what you actually need. Tell us about the project and we'll send you a clear quote before anything starts.

What's the difference between a case study video and a testimonial?

A testimonial is a customer saying nice things about you. A case study is a story: a real customer talks through a specific problem and how it was solved.

Can you film at our office, event or conference?

Yes. We film on location at offices, sites, stores and events. We bring the cameras, lighting and audio.

Do you work outside Melbourne?

We're based in Melbourne and work with organisations across Australia. We travel interstate when the job needs it.

Got a video in mind?

Tell us what you're after. No brief is too rough.
