Video podcast production, Melbourne

VIDPOD is a Melbourne podcast production company that makes video podcasts for brands, universities and businesses. We record, film and edit your podcast as a video show, in a studio or wherever you are, including your own office or boardroom. You get finished episodes, clips for social and one person looking after it from brief to delivery.

We've produced thousands of episodes, including The Numbers Game (five years and 300+ episodes) and Let's Talk Teaching for Monash University's Faculty of Education. Founder Tommy Jackett has sat on both sides of the mic, co-hosting 1,022 daily episodes of The Daily Talk Show.

At a glance

  • Who: VIDPOD, founded by Tommy Jackett
  • What: video podcast production, from show idea to published episodes and clips
  • Where: based in Melbourne, recording on location or remotely across Australia
  • Who for: brands, universities, financial services firms and organisations building a branded, corporate or internal podcast

What we do

Shape the show

Format, hosts and guests, the show name, and the look and sound. New shows start with our show development workshop.

Record and film

Cinema cameras and high-end audio, set up in your office, boardroom or HQ, in a studio, or recorded remotely with hosts and guests anywhere.

Edit and publish

Every episode edited as video and audio, plus show notes, intros, short clips for social, and getting it out on YouTube, Spotify and Apple Podcasts.

Private and internal shows

Member-only and internal podcasts for training, learning or customer education, made just as carefully as a public show.

What you get

Depending on the show, that usually includes:

  • One point of contact from brief to delivery
  • Show planning and content guidance for each episode
  • Filming and recording, with the cameras, lights and audio brought to you
  • A video edit of each episode for YouTube
  • An audio edit for Spotify and Apple Podcasts
  • Show notes and short vertical clips for social
  • Help with publishing and distribution

Who it's for

Brands, universities, financial services firms and organisations that want a show to build trust with the people they care about: customers, members, staff or students.

You don't need to have made a podcast before. We help you shape the show, then make recording day easy.

FAQ

What is video podcast production?

It's recording your podcast on camera as well as on the mic, so every episode works as a video show on YouTube and social, as well as on Spotify and Apple Podcasts. VIDPOD handles the whole thing: planning, filming, recording, editing and clips.

Can you record in our office or boardroom?

Yes. We bring the cameras, lights and audio and turn a room you already have into a set. We've recorded at Monash University, at Carlisle Homes HQ and on location for Help at Hand Education.

Do we need our own studio or gear?

No. We bring everything needed to record and film. All you need is a room and your hosts.

Can you record a podcast remotely?

Yes. Unconventional Business for HubSpot ANZ is recorded fully remotely, with hosts in New South Wales and Singapore and guests across Australia.

How much does a video podcast cost?

We quote each show on what it needs: the number of episodes, recording days, locations and what you want from each episode. Tell us about the show and we'll send you a clear quote.

We've never made a podcast. Can you help us start?

Yes. The hosts of The Numbers Game came to us with just the idea in 2021. We ran them through our show development workshop, then built the format, branding and process from there.
