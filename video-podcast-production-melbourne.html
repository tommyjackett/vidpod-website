FAQ

Something else on your mind? Ask us anything.

What do we get from video podcast production?

The world is going video first, and people now search YouTube the way they search Google. We film your show beautifully so it can be watched as well as heard, then build the system that turns each episode into searchable pages, clips and Shorts. Every conversation keeps getting found long after it goes out.

Can you record it in our office or boardroom?

Yes, absolutely. Even if your boardroom or office doesn't seem right, we bring the lighting, sound and know-how to make it look and sound great. We've recorded at Monash University, Carlisle Homes and Help at Hand Education, and in plenty of other offices, studios and boardrooms.

Can we make a podcast just for our staff?

Yes. Many of the shows we make are internal or members only, used for training, onboarding and sharing what your experts know. We can even build the setup behind it, so listening is easy and the way it reaches your team is world class.

Can you record a podcast remotely?

Yes. Several of the shows we produce are recorded completely remotely, like Unconventional Business for HubSpot ANZ, with hosts in New South Wales and Singapore and guests across Australia. We look after the setup and guide everyone through it, so all they need to do is talk.

What do we need to do?

Whether you've got the seed of an idea or want to workshop it with us, we'll help you work out what it takes and where we can support you. From there we plan the episodes, guests and content with you, and look after the recording, editing and clips. Whether you're the host, the brand or the business behind it, we make sure everything comes together.

How long does it take to launch a podcast?

Most shows go from first chat to first episode in a few weeks. We help you plan the first run of episodes up front, so you launch with a steady rhythm rather than one episode at a time.

Do you help find guests and topics?

Yes. We can plan topics with you, suggest guests your audience will want to hear from, and prepare each episode with notes and questions so your hosts feel ready.

How much does a video podcast cost?

We quote podcast video production on what each show needs: how many episodes, where you record and what you'd like made from each one. Tell us about the show and we'll send a clear quote before anything starts.
