FAQ

What is video podcast production?

It's recording your podcast on camera as well as on the mic, so every episode works as a video show on YouTube and social, as well as on Spotify and Apple Podcasts.

Can you record in our office or boardroom?

Yes. We've recorded at Monash University, at Carlisle Homes HQ and on location for Help at Hand Education.

Do we need our own studio or gear?

No. We bring everything needed to record and film. All you need is a room and your hosts.

Can you record a podcast remotely?

Yes. Unconventional Business for HubSpot ANZ is recorded fully remotely, with hosts in New South Wales and Singapore and guests across Australia.

How much does a video podcast cost?

We quote each show on what it needs. Tell us about the show and we'll send you a clear quote.

Tell us about it, even if it's just an idea.
