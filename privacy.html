Privacy Policy

Last updated 14 November 2026

VIDPOD Pty Ltd produces video and podcast content for its clients. To publish and manage that content, VIDPOD uses its own internal tools that connect to YouTube (through the YouTube API Services), Instagram and Simplecast.

What the tools access

Only the channels and accounts a client has authorised, to upload videos, set thumbnails, titles and descriptions, and read performance statistics.

What is stored

Sign-in tokens and statistics, kept securely on VIDPOD's own systems. No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Removing access

A client can remove VIDPOD's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, or in the platform's own settings. Stored data is deleted on request.

YouTube

These tools use the YouTube API Services. The YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy apply.
