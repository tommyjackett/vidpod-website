Built From the Ground Up

In 2021, the hosts came to us with the idea for The Numbers Game. We ran them through our show development workshop, then got to work building the show from the ground up: branding, format, process, strategy.

What started as an audio-only show has evolved into a well-established weekly video podcast. We help with content guidance and input, film every session on cinema cameras, and handle all post-production, social cutdowns, and distribution.

Five years in. 280+ episodes deep. A strong partnership and a well-oiled operation. There's something to be said for the compounding effect of pressing record week after week.

It's finance, investment and business — but not as you know it. Each week Jason Robinson (CPA Accountant) and Nick Reilly (Planner) come together to bring you strategy and advice around the importance of knowing your numbers in life and business.

Numbers really do tell a story, so let them help you make it a good one. From ETFs and super to property strategy, side hustles and starting a business — The Numbers Game cuts through the noise with real talk, no jargon.
