Made For Members

The Huddle is a conversation-style podcast we built with Help at Hand Education for their subscription platform. Exclusive to paying members, every episode is a sit-down with experts answering the big questions every parent worries about in those moments that really matter.

They had the perfect location and the experts lined up. We helped shape the show, brought the lights, cameras and audio, and made every conversation count.

Private podcast feeds like this are a powerful way for any business to deliver real value to its members or customers. We love getting to make them for the brands building real education for the people who need it.

The Huddle is life-saving health and first aid education for parents and caregivers of little humans.

Created by Help at Hand Education, it's a subscription platform of expert-led videos, checklists, and The Huddle's exclusive podcast: conversations with experts to help you feel confident in moments that matter most.
